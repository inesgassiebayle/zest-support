# Zest project management

The rules live in this repo. **Read them before giving a number** — do not answer from memory.

## Always read
- `skills/project-management.md` — Zest's sources, budget composition
  (66% level of effort), the last known cut, open audit findings and how to
  answer.
- `skills/project-management/evm-manual.md` — how to measure EV by
  activity type, diagnose, forecast (bottom-up ETC), check and defend a number.

## Read as the task requires
- `skills/project-management/course.md` — the course theory, the
  glossary and solved exercises, for study or exam questions.
- `docs/product.md` — scope, milestones, team and risks.
- `skills/multica.md` — ticket status, the input for the EV of
  development work.
- The `zest` app repo, if it is cloned next to this one: `kpis/README.md`
  (daily platform KPIs) and `git log` / merged PRs (what really shipped).
- The team's Drive (spreadsheet "10B-Equipo 02 - EVM y Curva S", "Guía EVM",
  "Memo_Respaldo_Gestion_Financiera_Zest", the steering decks) when a Google
  Drive connector is available.

## Non-negotiable
- Never invent or inflate PV, EV or AC: read them or ask. Say the source and the cut date.
- Report the global indices and the development-only indices side by side.
- Show the formula with the values, then what it means for Zest, the trend and
  the decision it calls for.
- Don't write to the spreadsheet or Multica unless the user asks.
- Answer in Spanish.
